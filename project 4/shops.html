Top 5 Card Shops in Southern Maryland

Swords and Spoon

A fanatasy theme card shop, that offers a variety of cards and table top games. Even host local card tornaments for various TGC's like Magic The Gathering, Pokemon, and One Piece. Another is table top gamees like DnD and Warhammer. They often run event for pre-release for pokemon sets, and you can sell cards and buy from them.

Ratings:

  1. Rodriques 5 out 5 stars:Swords & Spoons in California, Maryland is such a cool spot! The vibe is super friendly and laid-back, and everyone there clearly loves what they do. The staff are awesome, super helpful and easy to talk to AND they know their stuff when it comes to games. The store has a great selection of tabletop games, minis, and cards, and it’s all really well organized.
  2. Twait 5 out of 5 stars:Swords and Spoons is hands down the best local game store I’ve ever been to! The atmosphere is welcoming, the staff is friendly and knowledgeable, and there’s always something exciting happening. Whether you’re into card games, miniatures, board games, or tabletop RPGs, this place has it all.
  3. Kelly 5 out of 5 stars: A significant reinvention of the previous tenant at this location, High Tide Games. The vibe is amazing. It's cleaner, brighter, and overall a much cheerier place.
